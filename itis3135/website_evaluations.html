Website Evaluations

The sites below are judged using the four CRAP principles: Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity.

DoorDash

  • Contrast: They use a red orange accent against white backgrounds and dark text. Buttons and headlines stand out clearly from smaller supporting text.
  • Repetition: The same fonts, rounded buttons, colors, and card layouts are repeated across all pages. Restaurant cards all look alike, so the site is consistent and familiar.
  • Alignment: Cards, headlines, and the search bar sit on a grid with even spacing. The layout is organized and easy to go through.
  • Proximity: Related things are grouped together. The sign in and sign up button are next to eachother for example.

Daily Mail

  • Contrast: Most things are loud, with huge bold headlines, bright photos, and colored links all competing with each other. Since everything is emphasized, nothing stands out, and the smaller text is hard to read.
  • Repetition: Headline and thumbnail blocks are different sizes. Ads and sidebar content use their own fonts and colors, which breaks the consistency.
  • Alignment: Multiple columns, floating ads, and mismatched image sizes create uneven edges. There is no clear grid with content.
  • Proximity: Dozens of stories, links, and ads are crammed together with little free space. It is hard to tell which headline goes with which image, and ads blend in with articles.
